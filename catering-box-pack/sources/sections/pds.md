## 7. Performance tests (to sign off revision B)

| Test | Method | Pass criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Fit | 12 real bakes, 4 x 3, on the liner | Closes without touching the lid; no bake crushed [CONFIRM] |
| Vent hold | Filled hot, 30 minutes at serving temperature | Bases stay crisp; no pooling on the lid; board not softened [CONFIRM] |
| Liner strike-through | Oiliest bake (meat or cheese), 30 minutes hot | No grease through to the board [CONFIRM] |
| Stack | Filled hot, 5 high, 60 minutes at ambient; then a car-boot ride | No lid sag that touches food, no wall bulge, tuck holds [CONFIRM] |
| Print | Rub and scuff on the outer face; white opacity against the drawdown | No transfer to hands or bakes; opacity within the approved standard [CONFIRM] |
| Seal | Applied across the fold, 30 minutes hot | Stays down, no lift at the fold [CONFIRM] |
