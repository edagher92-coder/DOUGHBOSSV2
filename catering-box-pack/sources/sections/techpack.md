- Sizes follow the FEFCO convention: **internal** dimensions, L x B x H, measured on the erected box (FEFCO Code of Designs, read 3 October 2026 for the PDS).
- L = 385 mm runs along the front and back walls. B = 290 mm runs along the side walls. The 290 mm walls carry the vents.

- Board caliper used in the working die: t = 1.6 mm (E-flute) [SUPPLIER TO PROPOSE: your measured caliper].
