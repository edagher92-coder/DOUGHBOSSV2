### Who buys

These are the occasions the live site names (VERIFIED). The buyer profiles come from `04-corporate-outreach.md` §2 (INFERRED there):

| Occasion (live site wording) | Who actually buys | What they are judged on (INFERRED) |

| "Family gatherings" / "celebrations" | The host: a family member feeding relatives | Generous food that looks good on the table and needs no plating |
| "Functions" | Event coordinator, community or school organiser | A supplier that fits the run sheet, with clear dietary information |
| "Footy nights" | The host of a game-night gathering at home or at a club | Easy food for a crowd that can sit out and be shared |

Sources: home page "Office runs, footy nights and functions."; catering page "Office runs, family gatherings, celebrations and functions" and "Fresh food for meetings, teams and celebrations." (both VERIFIED 2026-10-03).

### The job to be done (INFERRED)

> When I am the person feeding a group, I want food that turns up looking generous and is easy to put out, so the table looks full, everyone is fed, and I look good, without plating anything or chasing anyone.

The live site's own line states this job in four words: **"Feed the whole table."** (VERIFIED). This plan uses it as the campaign line because it is the owner's published wording and it makes no stakes claim.

### Positioning statement (internal, not copy)

For people in the south-west who feed a group, Dough Boss catering is a contemporary Lebanese bakery's bakes, sent out in a black box that stacks to the size of the group and opens on the table with the bakes revealed last. The nearest Lebanese competitors present as "restaurant plus catering" (Al Aseel, Sammy's) or as pizza-first chains (OOSHMAN). Dough Boss is the bakery itself (competitors.md §1 and keyword-themes.md, INFERRED there). **[CONFIRM: the ledger entry for "Lebanese bakery" / "A contemporary Lebanese bakery." before any paid or printed use.]**

What makes this true, and so safe to lean on:

- It is a bakery with three named shops: Revesby, Bankstown and Roselands Centro (VERIFIED).
- The box exists and anyone can see it. A visible, physical difference needs no superlative.
- Catering is sold by the dozen in named packages: Morning Tea, Office Platter and Function Spread (site data, per the pack brief). Package prices on the site are INDICATIVE and never appear in this plan's materials.

What it must not lean on: "best", "authentic", "halal", "gluten free", "allergen-free", delivery areas, lead times, minimum orders, capacity, ratings or review counts. Each is unconfirmed or banned (compliance §1, §7; claims.json open claims).

### How the box carries the brand

1. **The box is the ad in the room.** A catering order is eaten in front of people who did not buy it. The box is the one brand touchpoint those people see. It carries the "DOUGH BOSS." wordmark, the website and @doughboss, and nothing that needs substantiating.
2. **Stacked means scale.** One dozen box per dozen ordered means the stack height shows the order size: Morning Tea is 2 boxes, Office Platter 3 and Function Spread 5. This works only if one box holds exactly one dozen. **[CONFIRM: fit test with a real sample. Until then no public piece says "12 per box" or "2 boxes".]**
3. **"Open it up with the bites at the end."** (Elie's direction.) Black outside, golden bakes on a paper liner inside. The reveal is the content moment for photos and reels (section 4).
4. **Personal.** The kraft seal is filled in by hand with FOR, DATE, a tick list (cheese, za'atar, meat, spinach) and BOX __ OF __. This is the "personal, real, not perfect" brief made physical. It also helps operations: a host can match boxes to bakes.
5. **The reorder loop.** The seal or liner carries a UTM'd catering link (section 5). The person who opens the box can enquire next time without searching.

## 4. Content and photography

### 4.1 Shoot rules (from the owner's direction and the brief)

- **Dark, real, high contrast, personal, expressive.** "Professional but real and not perfect."
- **No people and no hands** in any shot. **No smoke, steam or haze. No lemons.** No props or garnishes that are not in the box the customer receives. Show the real bakes in the real count **[CONFIRM count]** (compliance §1: images must show what is supplied).
- **Real only.** Photograph the produced box with food baked that day. No AI renders, no stock, no retouching that changes the food's colour, size or count. Clean-up of dust on the backdrop is fine. Keep the box's own imperfections: fibre, ink sitting in the card, fold cracking, flour dust, the grease spot on the liner.
- **Light (INFERRED craft guidance):** one hard key light from the side, black flags for deep shadow, and a small warm fill only inside the box so the bakes glow against the black. Shoot RAW with a colour reference card in frame for the first frame of each setup, so the ember red (#e2231a) and the cream read true.
- **Surfaces:** dark timber, black steel, worn concrete or the shop's own counter.
- **Formats:** shoot each hero for 4:5 (Instagram feed), 9:16 (Reels and Stories), 1:1 (GBP) and 16:9 (website hero; the pack brief makes the approved box the reference for the home-page hero). Leave space around the subject for crops.

## 5. Measurement: the tracking already built, and how the box ties in

### What the `doughboss-growth` companion plugin already does (read from its code and readme, 2026-10-03)

Every feature ships switched off. Each needs its prerequisite, such as the consent banner before Tag Manager (readme.txt).

- **Consent banner and Tag Manager loader.** Tags fire only after consent.
- **First-party attribution** (`includes/attribution/`). On landing, `dbgr-attribution.js` reads the UTM parameters, click ids (gclid, gbraid, wbraid, fbclid, msclkid), the referrer host and the landing path. It keeps them in the first-party cookie `dbgr_attr` (90 days, 1.5 KB at most). UTMs, referrer and landing path are kept **only with measurement consent**. Click ids need **advertising consent**. Without consent nothing is written. On `doughboss_catering_enquiry_created` it writes one `lead_meta` row per enquiry: segment, company, landing key, attribution, marketing consent and the consent wording version. The admin screen lists each lead's utm source, medium and campaign.
- **Lead form segments** (`includes/leads/`): `corporate`, `office_breakfast` and `events`, matching the landing pages `catering-corporate`, `catering-office-breakfast` and `catering-events` (`content/landing/*.json`). Those pages show `[CONFIRM]` claim blocks for service area, delivery and lead time. Nothing renders until each claim is confirmed.
- **Server-side conversions** (`includes/conversions/`). Each catering enquiry queues one `generate_lead` (form `catering_enquiry`, no value, event_id `lead:{enquiry_number}`) to GA4 (needs measurement consent) and to Meta as `Lead` (needs advertising consent). It also produces a weekly Google Ads offline-conversion CSV. No name, email, phone or notes go into any payload.
- **Browser event taxonomy** (`content/events.json`):
  - `generate_lead` (params: form, category, guest_band, store)
  - `quote_step`
  - `cta_click` (cta, destination)
  - `click_to_call` and `get_directions` (surface includes `catering_page`)
  - The neutral `coming_soon_view` and `waitlist_submit` events stay separate from catering.
- **Known gap in the code:** if Tag Manager also sends `generate_lead` from the browser, GA4 counts the lead twice. Keep one source (the code carries this as a `[CONFIRM]` gap).
