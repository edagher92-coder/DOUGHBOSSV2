# Status, gates and how to use this pack

**Pack version:** v1, 3 October 2026. **Status: CONCEPT FOR DEVELOPMENT.** Internal only. Nothing in this pack is to be printed in volume, published or shown to customers until the gates below are cleared.

## What this pack is

The working brand and catering pack for Dough Boss. It sets out the catering "dozen box" and everything the brand needs around it: identity, structure and dieline, print specification, technical data, photography and website masters, costing, merchandise, marketing, production documentation, governance and expansion. It is written so a converter, a printer, a photographer, a web developer and a new shop manager can each work from it without a briefing call.

## Gates before anything goes public

1. **Packaging is real.** The owner confirms in writing that the produced box matches this artwork and structure. Until then every photograph is labelled "concept" and is not presented as the product (Australian Consumer Law, overall impression).
2. **Real food.** The bakes in every image are AI stand-ins. They are replaced with photographs of the real bakes before publication, and a Lebanese baker reviews the toppings for authenticity.
3. **Wordmark and trade mark.** The wordmark is the live site's. The ownership and trade-mark position is open (decisions register, Part D). No volume print until it is settled.
4. **Claims.** Only the verified lines in Section 2 may print. "Since 2009", "Fresh from the oven" and "Three shops baking daily" carry a [CONFIRM] before permanent print.
5. **Physical tests.** Fit (12 real bakes), vent hold, liner strike-through, stack (hot, 5 high) and lid sag, as listed in the PDS.
6. **Quotes.** The real specification (black board, opaque white plus ember, custom 0427 size) has no published price. Order nothing until written quotes are in.

## Conventions used throughout

- **[CONFIRM: ...]** marks a fact still to be confirmed by the owner, a supplier or a test. **[QUOTE]** marks a price that has to be requested. **REGULATOR-CHECK** marks a point for NSW Food Authority or FSANZ.
- **OBSERVED** facts were read from a cited source on the stated date. **INFERRED** facts are reasoning from those sources.
- Prices are AUD. USD figures are converted at the dated RBA rate and flagged as estimates; a card charge converts at the bank's own rate on the day.
- No price, date, specification or stock level in this pack is invented. The appendix lists every open item found in the pack.
