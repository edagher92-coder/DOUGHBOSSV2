# Dough Boss catering box: marketing pack brief (internal)

## Outcome
A production-ready Dough Boss catering box that could go to a printer and be photographed in the shop:
structure, dieline (print-ready vector PDF at 1:1), artwork for every panel, liner and seal label,
a packaging data sheet (PDS), and photoreal photography of that exact box. The approved pack then
becomes the reference for the home-page hero. Catering is the hero product: no shortfall.
Gate: every specialist on the panel ranks the pack 8.5/10 or higher.

## Working structure (proposal, all sizes [CONFIRM])
- A "dozen box": one-piece corrugated mailer with a hinged lid and tuck front (FEFCO 0427 style),
  12 bakes in a 4 x 3 layout, with side vents for heat. Orders then stack: 2 dozen = 2 boxes,
  3 dozen = 3 boxes ("stacked"). Working internal size 385 x 290 x 50 mm, assuming about 9 cm
  bakes [CONFIRM: measure a mini and do a fit test with a sample].
- Outer: black kraft liner, natural brown kraft inside. Print: 2 spot colours (opaque white + ember red).
  Greaseproof liner sheet inside. Round kraft seal label across the front, handwritten fields.

## Look
Dark, high contrast, expressive type, warmth inside (golden bakes on a paper liner), personal touches
(handwritten marker on the seal: "For ___", the date, a tick list), real-world imperfection (fibre,
ink sitting in the card, slight misregistration, fold-line cracking, flour dust, a grease spot on the
liner), professional structure and print.
