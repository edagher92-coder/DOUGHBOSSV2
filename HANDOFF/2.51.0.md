### What changed

1. **Store-first ordering** (`public/js/doughboss.js`, `doughboss-shop-status.js`, css). On multi-shop sites the customer picks a shop before the menu (open/closed badge, today's hours, prep time). "Change store" re-checks the cart via `POST /cart/revalidate` and removes items the new shop doesn't sell. Not gated on capacity mode.
2. **Pickup scheduling** (new `includes/class-doughboss-pickup.php`; `class-doughboss-capacity.php`; `class-doughboss-locations.php`).
   - `GET /pickup/options`, `POST /pickup/hold` (optional `release_token`), `POST /pickup/release`. Nonce-gated; per-IP hold limiter that honours the trusted-proxy setting.
   - ASAP = first available slot while the shop is open; scheduled = 15-minute slots for today plus `pickup_days_ahead` days, last slot starts `cutoff_minutes` before close. Lead time = prep + busy extra. Default 6 orders per slot.
   - Holds last 10 minutes and are re-checked at `/payment-intent` (`peek_hold`) and converted inside the order transaction (`lock_hold_for_checkout`).
   - A **paid order is never refused for capacity**: an expired hold is re-acquired, a full slot is kept with `capacity_overflow=1`, a passed slot is shifted to now + prep. Each case leaves a `[SYSTEM]` note. A paid order whose hold can't be attached at all is saved untimed with a note.
   - Cancelling an order releases its hold.
3. **Checkout** (`class-doughboss-rest-controller.php`, `class-doughboss-order.php`).
   - The pickup facts travel in the immutable snapshot `payload.order`, so every order writer (hosted Square, webhook recovery, approved, `/checkout`) inherits them.
   - Binding and checkout-key hashes add pickup keys **only when a time is selected**, so in-flight 2.50 attempts survive the upgrade.
4. **Square** (`class-doughboss-square-orders.php`). Timed orders get `ticket_name` (≤30 chars) and metadata `db_pickup_at` and `db_pickup_mode`. There are no fulfilments (see 2.46 handoff). The filter `doughboss_square_pickup_ticket` turns it off.
5. **Kitchen board** (`doughboss-orderboard.js`/css).
   - An Upcoming section holds scheduled orders until `fire_at_utc` (= pickup − prep). They don't alarm early.
   - ASAP and Scheduled badges, pickup time on each card, a single Accept for timed orders, and "+10 min" (`POST /admin/order/{id}/delay`, compare-and-swap on version).
   - The delay fires `doughboss_order_delayed`, which sends an email and SMS.
6. **Admin** (DoughBoss > Shops): Capacity mode Off/Shadow/**Live**, orders per slot, days ahead, cut-off, busy extra minutes, pause ordering, holiday closures.
7. **Notifications.** The placed email, accepted email, delayed email and SMS, kitchen ticket, confirmation page and tracking page all show "Pickup at <shop>, <time>". Scheduled or far-off orders get "ready for pickup: Tomorrow 7:30am" instead of "about 1243 minutes" (built-in template only).
8. **Concurrency fixes**, both found on real MariaDB:
   - `Order::create` takes the settings-row lock before its gap-locking replay lookups. Without it, concurrent checkouts deadlocked and showed a bogus `doughboss_staff_approval_required`. **This is also present in live 2.50.**
   - `create_hold` no longer takes gap locks (plain idempotency read, then locks by primary key; the sweep works by primary key).
9. Version 2.51.0, readme changelog.

### Known limitations

- Stripe-hosted cancel and return with a hold older than 10 minutes means the customer must reload the page. Stripe is not the active gateway.
- ASAP orders can sit in Upcoming for up to one slot length, and "+10 min" on a pending timed order can move it back to Upcoming. Brief kitchen staff on this.
- Two tabs can hold two slots for one cart for up to 10 minutes. This heals itself on expiry.
- A zero-discount voucher can drop a paid order's hold to "untimed with note".
- `/locations` makes 2 extra queries per shop.
- `DoughBoss_Locations::update` re-derives the slug from the name (pre-existing).
