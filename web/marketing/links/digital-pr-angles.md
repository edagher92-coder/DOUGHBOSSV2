## Ground rules

- Earned coverage only. No paid links, no link swaps, no link schemes, no fake accounts, no fake reviews or testimonials, and no invented quotes or "customers say" lines.
- A pitch states only facts that are confirmed in the claims ledger (src/content/ledger.ts). If a fact is not confirmed, the pitch waits.
- No superlatives, ratings, review counts, prices, offers, delivery promises, lead times or capacity in a pitch unless the ledger confirms them.
- Pitch real people at real contact routes. A journalist's published email or the outlet's contact form. One relevant pitch per outlet. If an outlet's pitch email is a commercial electronic message, it needs a sender block and an unsubscribe line (compliance-au.md section 4). Do not use bought or scraped lists.
- Tenant separation: Dough Boss is its own business. Nothing in this file draws on any Snow Flow or Slushie Co material.
- Sources of the supportable facts below: the live doughboss.com.au pages read on 2026-10-02 through a summarising fetch tool (quotes may be imprecise, so re-read each page before use), the owner's earlier published pages in /home/user/DOUGHXSNOW/web, the voucher project report /home/user/DOUGHXSNOW/docs/PROJECT_REPORT.md, and src/lib/data/catalogue.ts.
