# Pre-send checklist: outreach email, LinkedIn message, phone call, printed sheet

INTERNAL. One page. Run it on every batch before anything goes out. A second person reads the item against this list and initials it. Practical guidance, not legal advice. Full guide: `docs/marketing/research/compliance-au.md`. Playbook: `docs/marketing/04-corporate-outreach.md`.

## 1. Permission to send (Spam Act 2003)

- [ ] Every recipient has an evidence record: the URL where the address is published, the date read, the role, and a note on why catering is relevant to that role.
- [ ] The address is a role or office-holder address that enables the public to contact that role, conspicuously published, with no "no unsolicited commercial messages" statement nearby. Or the person gave express consent.
- [ ] The message is relevant to the recipient's role. No generic accounts or unrelated roles.
- [ ] No purchased, rented or scraped list. No address-harvesting tool. No guessed addresses.
- [ ] Enquiry-form leads are not in the outbound sequence (an enquiry is not marketing consent).
- [ ] Suppression list checked: unsubscribed, asked-to-stop and bounced addresses removed.

## 2. The message

- [ ] Sender identified: name, role, business name, legal entity and ABN, street or postal address, a phone number that is answered.
- [ ] A clear, conspicuous unsubscribe statement with a working address, which will receive replies for at least 30 days after sending.
- [ ] The footer says where the address was found.
- [ ] One clear ask. One link at most.
- [ ] Subject is 60 characters or fewer and says what the email is about.
- [ ] No placeholder left unfilled (`{...}`) and no `[CONFIRM` marker anywhere in the text.

## 3. Claims (Australian Consumer Law and the claims ledger)

- [ ] Every fact is shop data from `catalogue.ts` or a claim confirmed in the ledger with a source. Otherwise it is out.
- [ ] No superlatives or comparatives (best, number one, cheapest, leading, famous, award-winning, authentic).
- [ ] No halal, organic, "fresh daily", "never frozen" or gluten-free claim unless confirmed in the ledger and cleared.
- [ ] No prices, "from" figures, discounts, free offers, minimums, lead times, capacity or delivery area.
- [ ] No tasting is mentioned unless Elie has approved cost and terms and the wording is in the ledger.
- [ ] No testimonial, rating or review count unless real, current and permitted.
- [ ] No unannounced product is named, hinted at or teased in outreach (see `docs/site/teaser-direction.md`); any message about family or party occasions speaks to parents and organisers, not children.

## 4. Channel rules

- [ ] Email: sent individually from a named inbox, not from the orders domain. SPF, DKIM and DMARC set. `[CONFIRM: sending domain]`
- [ ] LinkedIn: typed by the account holder. No automation, no extension, no scraping. Connection note is 300 characters or fewer and has no pitch.
- [ ] Phone: weekdays inside normal business hours (the Do Not Call Register page lists weekdays 9am to 8pm and Saturdays 9am to 5pm; never a Sunday or public holiday as a working rule). Caller identification on. Mobiles and sole-trader numbers washed against the Do Not Call Register. Call ends when asked.
- [ ] Printed sheet: customer-facing part only; internal checklist removed; hours re-checked.

## 5. Privacy and data

- [ ] Only the minimum data is recorded. No personal data about private individuals.
- [ ] Dietary needs are collected as counts and kept out of ad platforms, email tools and tracking pixels.
- [ ] No customer list is uploaded to an ad platform without consent and a privacy-policy disclosure.
- [ ] Privacy policy is live and names the data collected, the purposes and overseas platforms. `[CONFIRM]`

## 6. Stop and escalate

- [ ] A stop request is actioned the same day: suppress the address and number across every channel, and mark the record `LOST` with reason `unsubscribed`. The outer limit under ACMA guidance is an EXTRACT only (5 business days). `[CONFIRM: ACMA]`
- [ ] Any spam complaint pauses the sequence until reviewed.
- [ ] Items for a lawyer or regulator are logged in section 11 of the playbook and cleared in writing before the first send: current Spam Act compilation and Regulations, LinkedIn direct messages, Industry Standard text, catering terms, gifts and hospitality at public bodies.

## 7. Sign-off

- [ ] Elie has given an explicit yes for this batch in this session. Nothing is sent without it.
