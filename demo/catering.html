Catering Q&A

Good to know

How much notice should I give?

As early as you can. Lead time depends on the date, quantity and menu mix, and is confirmed before acceptance.

Where is catering prepared?

Catering is prepared through Revesby for now. Ask the team what collection or other arrangement is available.

Can you help with dietary requirements?

Tell us about dietary needs and allergies. We can explain suitable choices, but our kitchen handles common allergens and cannot promise an allergen-free environment.

How do I pay?

The team will confirm the price and payment arrangement. Online catering deposits are not live yet.

Can I change or cancel?

Call 0422 487 487 as soon as possible. Changes depend on preparation already completed.
