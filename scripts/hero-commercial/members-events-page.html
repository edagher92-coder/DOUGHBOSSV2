Your favourites, a tap away

Dough Boss on your phone.

Save Dough Boss to your home screen for a quick way back to the menu, your order and this hub. You’ll need an internet connection; saving the app doesn’t switch on online ordering or membership.

If your browser offers installation, a button will appear here. You can also use the steps below.

iPhone or iPad

Open Dough Boss in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add. If you don’t see the option, check Safari’s sharing menu.

Android phone

Open Dough Boss in Chrome. Tap the three-dot menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen. The wording depends on your browser.

Finish any checkout in the browser where you started it before opening the saved app. Never pay again just because an app or browser shows an uncertain result—contact your shop.
