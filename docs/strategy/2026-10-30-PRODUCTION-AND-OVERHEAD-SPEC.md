# DoughBoss central kitchen, A-Z production, cost-out and store diligence
Status: development / operating design; real kitchen is at Revesby with four cooks according to owner, currently near capacity. Second kitchen in shared warehouse is a PROPOSED contingency, not automatically licensed or ready. Owner reports meat from affiliated butcher, in-house bread/dough and Snowflow stock/equipment at cost. Verify fully loaded economics and relevant food rules.

## A-Z production procedure
A ingest real paid orders + capacity-approved contracts; B forecast demand by SKU/half-hour/store; C freeze versioned recipe/BOM, allergen and yield; D check inventory/reorder and buy authorised ingredients; E receive with PO, quantity, batch/temperature and supplier acceptance; F segregate raw/ready-to-eat, allergens, FEFO; G clean and calibrate, open shift safety checklist; H weigh flour/water and mix/proof dough against recipe; I prepare meat and fillings with butchery lot, cooked-loss and cross-contamination tracking; J form/portion minis to standard weights; K add toppings and dietary controls; L apply validated cook process; M apply validated cooling/holding (potentially hazardous food generally <=5C cold or >=60C hot in storage and transport absent safe alternative); N package with safe label, lot, use-by and handling rules; O count/weigh/QC sample and block defects; P pick orders by confirmed store and slot; Q prepare electronic batch/lot dispatch manifest; R log actual courier/vehicle route and cold-chain exceptions; S receive at store with count/temp/damage; T store within validated temperature/shelf life; U finish/serve using approved final processes, noting 1-2 staff assumption remains unproven; V verify customer dietary/portion/order; W dispatch or set up event / Snowflow equipment; X settle actual Square/WordPress and Xero records by entity/location; Y complaint, recall and credit with lot tracing; Z daily huddle and weekly cost/quality improvement.
For each station capture operator, timestamp, units accepted/rejected, reason code, rework, temperatures where relevant, duration and version. Mock recall and vehicle/cooling failure exercises before full-scale rollout.

## Real economic costs - intercompany and overhead
Cost-price meat and Snowflow products are not free: account for direct material, butcher labour/service, transportation, shrinkage, kitchen staff, rent, power, storage, depreciation, maintenance, packaging, vehicle, insurance, card fee and account service. Record transfer prices between legal entities and accountant-approved allocations. Cost-ledger must expose cost vs market benchmark for related-party goods; no hidden subsidies to make DoughBoss look profitable.
Claimed 400-500k saving/shop is replacement-capex *avoidance relative to a fresh build*, not verified cash profit or valuation. Four shops at this claimed differential imply indicative 1.6-2.0m avoided build budget ONLY if counterfactual and fit-out condition verified. Inspect equipment service history, ownership/security interests/PPSR, electrical/fire/extraction, allowed use, make-good and necessary upgrades.

## 12 cost and efficiency experiments
1 portion-control weighing per recipe, 2 batch yield and cooked-meat shrinkage, 3 suppliers benchmark and three quotes for non-affiliated goods, 4 consolidated purchase orders vs spoilage from case size, 5 standard packaging families and delivery protection, 6 labour minutes per 100 acceptable minis/station, 7 roster demand by half-hour plus lawful breaks/leave/super/penalties, 8 peak-line balancing and reduced changeover, 9 route density and cost/drop, 10 fridge seals/energy and preventive maintenance, 11 kitchen equipment utilisation and approved spare capacity, 12 channel contribution including marketplace fees, discounts and returns. Baseline for 30 days; experimental goals (NOT predicted outcomes): waste/unit -5 to -15%, labour minutes/unit -5 to -12%, box/unit -5 to -10%, route cost/drop -5 to -15%, each subject to quality/safety constraints. Measure absolute AUD gains and confidence.

## Technical acceptance
A batch can be traced ingredient lot -> produced SKU batch -> location -> catering order; unsafe exception blocks release; each fulfilled order has cost snapshot and actual distribution cost; supplier purchase matching/variance; signed monthly KPI review covering waste, minutes/100, utility/unit, cost/drop, complaints and fully allocated contribution. No unverified automatic purchasing or live kitchen changes.
