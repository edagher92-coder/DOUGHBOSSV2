## Candidate use, boundaries and gaps

- Independently design company-to-contact links and an attributed, dated activity history around the existing catering enquiry, with a clear authority for each record and tests for permission, input validation and duplicate operations. Do not infer uniqueness from email or domain alone; the reference imposes active uniqueness that may be unsuitable for real customers and shared addresses. Retain consent provenance and avoid storing unnecessary data.
- A follow-up task can have an owner, due date and completion state; the reference models these in Activity. Determine whether DoughBoss needs a distinct task record through the existing requirements before choosing persistence. Existing quote and enquiry records remain authoritative; no competing payment engine or portal.
- The reviewed CRM files do not establish a suitable WordPress role boundary, transactional outbox, Telegram verification, retry or idempotency design. Defer these to focused design and tests. Treat chat IDs and bot tokens as sensitive; authenticate and authorise inbound commands, keep provider requests behind a service boundary, and do not send customer data or trigger financial actions without specific authorisation. No Telegram provider or production capability is established by this research.
- The supplied [CRM topic](https://github.com/topics/crm) is discovery rather than evidence of suitability. No additional candidate was selected: the pinned CRM already gives the requested relationship and timeline examples, and no inspected topic project establishes the missing WordPress/Telegram requirements. Do not interpret this as an exhaustive survey.
