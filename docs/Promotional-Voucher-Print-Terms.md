# DoughBoss $5 promotional voucher — print and QR terms

Use this wording only after management sets and verifies the exact expiry date.
Do not print a batch with a blank, hidden or changing expiry.

> **$5 promotional discount**
>
> Minimum spend $3. One voucher per eligible person and allocation period.
> One use only. Valid at participating DoughBoss locations and online until
> **[DD Month YYYY]**. Show the QR/code before payment. Not redeemable for cash,
> no change given, not transferable, and cannot be combined with another offer
> unless DoughBoss says otherwise. Lost, copied or altered vouchers are void.

For a student allocation, add: “Eligible .edu or .edu.au email required; one
voucher per student for this allocation.” For the Bankstown hospital construction
worker allocation, replace that line with the management-approved eligibility
check and the same one-per-person allocation rule.

## Till control

Staff must complete the POS sale first, type the completed receipt number into
Voucher Scan, then scan the voucher. The dashboard records the signed-in cashier
and receipt reference. The named reconciliation owner compares every daily
redemption with the matching till receipt and signs off exceptions before close.

If staff scan the wrong in-store voucher, a DoughBoss manager can reverse that
single scan only after entering a reason. The original receipt reference and the
manager's audit entry remain on file. Online or order-linked redemptions cannot
be manually re-opened: use the original payment/order refund procedure instead.

## Important pricing outcome

The stated $3 minimum qualifies a $3 basket. Because the voucher is worth up to
$5, it can reduce that basket to $0 (with no change given). If management does
not want a fully free transaction, raise the printed and configured minimum spend
above $5 before issuing or printing vouchers.

This is promotional-voucher operational wording, not legal advice. Before print,
management should have the final promotion terms and expiry confirmed for the
actual campaign and distribution method.
